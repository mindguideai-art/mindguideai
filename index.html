Questions

Good to know

Who is MindGuide AI for?

Anyone in Nigeria who wants private, professional support for their mental health: students, working professionals, parents, couples and families. Whatever you’re going through, you’re welcome here.

How do I start?

Create an account, answer a few short questions about how you’re feeling, and take the 2-minute wellbeing assessment. You’ll see therapists who specialise in what you’re going through, then choose a plan and book a time.

Is it really private?

Yes. Calls connect directly between you and your therapist and are encrypted. Your registration details and assessment are only shared with a therapist if you allow it, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, from your sessions page, up to 24 hours before a confirmed session. You’ll get email and SMS reminders before every session.

Is MindGuide AI for emergencies?

No. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.
