About us

No one should navigate their mental wellbeing alone

MindGuide AI is a technology-enabled mental health and wellbeing platform designed to make mental healthcare more accessible, affordable, personalised and inclusive.

Why we exist

Every mental health journey is different

We recognise that mental health journeys are different. Everyone experiences emotional and psychological challenges differently, and finding the right support can often be difficult due to cost, limited access to professionals, stigma and a lack of reliable information.

MindGuide AI addresses these challenges by combining artificial intelligence, professional mental health services, education and community support to create a more connected approach to mental wellbeing.

What we offer

One connected place for your wellbeing

AI-assisted assessments

Wellbeing assessments with personalised recommendations, so you know where to start.

Qualified professionals

Connect with verified mental health professionals by video or voice, from wherever you are.

Educational resources

Reliable information to help you understand mental health and look after yourself.

Supportive communities

Connect with others who understand, in a safe and supportive space.

Programmes and events

Discover relevant mental health programmes and events that help you grow.

Strategic partnerships

Building partnerships with hospitals, NGOs and institutions to strengthen referrals, counselling and community support.

Beyond digital access

Working with partners who share our goal

Our approach extends beyond digital access. Through strategic partnerships with hospitals, healthcare professionals, NGOs, educational institutions and other relevant organisations, we aim to strengthen access to mental health education, professional referrals, counselling and community-based support.

What we believe

Technology should complement human care, not replace it.

At MindGuide AI, our goal is to bridge the gap between individuals who need support and the people, services and resources that can help them.

We are building an inclusive mental health ecosystem where no one has to navigate their mental wellbeing journey without guidance, connection or support.

MindGuide AI · Guiding minds… empowering women

Our mission

To make mental health and wellbeing support more accessible, affordable and personalised by connecting individuals to the right professional care, resources, communities and support systems through technology, education and strategic partnerships.

We are committed to creating a safe and supportive environment where everyone can access guidance, build emotional resilience and take meaningful steps towards better mental wellbeing.

Our vision

To become a leading technology-driven mental health and wellbeing ecosystem that transforms how individuals access, experience and sustain mental healthcare across Africa and beyond.

We envision a world where seeking mental health support is accessible, affordable, stigma-free and integrated into everyday life.

What we stand for

Our promises to you

Safety first

Every therapist’s licence is checked before they’re listed. If an assessment suggests someone may be at risk, they see emergency contacts straight away and the answers are flagged for our team to review.

Your privacy, your choice

Calls are encrypted and connect directly between you and your therapist. You decide which therapists can see your details, and you can stop sharing anytime.

Care without judgement

Whatever you’re going through, you deserve to be heard with respect, warmth and confidentiality.

Stigma-free support

Seeking help is a strength. We make it private and simple so you can take the first step on your own terms.

How we keep you safe

Built with security in mind

  • Therapists are verified by our team before they can see patients
  • Encrypted video and voice calls
  • Consent controls for who can see your information
  • Two-step sign-in for every admin account
  • Secure payments through Paystack. We never see your card details
