About MindGuide AI

Mental health care that meets you where you are

MindGuide AI is a Nigerian online therapy platform. We connect people, especially women, with licensed therapists through private video and voice sessions, and use simple, careful technology to make the first step easier.

Our mission

Guiding minds, empowering women.

Too many people go without support because therapy feels expensive, far away or hard to talk about. Women often carry the most and ask for help the least.

We’re changing that by making care private, affordable and available from a phone, with professionals our team has verified, and plans that start at ₦10,000 a month.

What we stand for

Our promises to you

Safety first

Every therapist’s licence is checked before they’re listed. If an assessment shows someone may be at risk, our team is alerted and the person sees emergency contacts straight away.

Your privacy, your choice

Calls are encrypted and connect directly between you and your therapist. You decide which therapists can see your details, and you can stop sharing anytime.

Care without judgement

Whatever you’re going through, you deserve to be heard with respect, warmth and confidentiality.

Technology that helps, not replaces

Our AI helps you find the right therapist and understand how you’re doing. Your care always comes from a qualified human professional.

How we keep you safe

Built with security in mind

  • Therapists are verified by our team before they can see patients
  • Encrypted video and voice calls
  • Consent controls for who can see your information
  • Two-step sign-in for every admin account
  • Secure payments through Paystack. We never see your card details
