Billing questions

How much does it cost?

Plans start at ₦10,000 a month for 2 sessions. Standard is ₦20,000 for 4 sessions, Premium is ₦30,000 for 8, and Family is ₦50,000 for 12 sessions shared across your household.

How do I pay?

Securely with Paystack, by card, bank transfer or USSD. We never see or store your card details.

Can I cancel my plan?

Yes, anytime from your Subscription page. You keep your plan until the end of the month you paid for, and it won’t renew.

What happens if I use all my sessions?

You can upgrade your plan at any time to get more sessions that month. Unused sessions don’t roll over.
