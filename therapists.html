How to join

Verified before you see your first client

We check every application so patients can trust everyone on MindGuide AI.

  1. Create your account and complete your profile: title, bio, specialties, languages, experience and fee.
  2. Upload your licence and any qualifications or certificates.
  3. Our team reviews them and approves your account, usually within a few working days.
  4. Set your hours and start accepting bookings.
